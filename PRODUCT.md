# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Vietnamese early-career users: 2nd/3rd-year university students and new graduates with under ~5 years of experience. They are unsure of their career direction, easily swayed by trends, and cannot afford or access a human mentor. They open OpenPath in short, anxious sessions, between classes, late at night before an application deadline, asking questions they'd be embarrassed to ask a professor. Job to be done: understand myself, find a direction that actually fits me, and know the next concrete step.

Secondary: companies hiring early-career talent who want lower recruiting cost and better-fit candidates.

## Product Purpose

OpenPath is a personalised AI career coach. It helps users understand their current profile, see realistic career opportunities ranked against the real market, compare where they are versus where they want to be, and follow a concrete action plan. Success is a user who makes a career decision from evidence about themselves and the market, not from trend-chasing, and who comes back weekly.

## Brand Personality

Grounded, warm, honest. It talks like a good older mentor: encouraging but never flattering, willing to push back on a bad idea with data. Never hype, never gamified, never corporate-HR. Vietnamese-first voice, natural not translated.

## Anti-references

- Trend-chasing edtech and "career quiz" apps: confetti, gamified badges, personality-test astrology.
- Enterprise HR dashboards: dense grids of KPIs where a person's future is a widget.
- Generic SaaS chrome: hero-metric templates, identical card grids, cream/beige AI default palettes.
- Chatbot bolted onto an app as a support-widget afterthought.

## Design Principles

1. **The conversation is the product.** Coaching happens in dialogue; every other screen is an artifact the conversation produced or reads from. The chat gets the space and the polish of a first-class AI tool, not a support widget.
2. **Evidence over encouragement.** Every claim shows its basis, market data, the user's own profile, a specific gap. Motivation without evidence is what the product exists to replace.
3. **One next step, always visible.** A user who feels lost must never leave a screen without knowing what to do next.
4. **Calm under uncertainty.** These users are anxious about their future. Restraint, quiet surfaces, and generous space are functional requirements, not taste.
5. **Honest about distance.** Show the real gap to a goal, including when it is large. Never flatter the user's readiness.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA. Body text ≥4.5:1. Full keyboard operability, especially in the chat composer and multi-step onboarding. `prefers-reduced-motion` honoured for all bar fills, message entrances, and typing indicators. Vietnamese diacritics must never clip: line-height and font choice account for stacked tone marks. Mobile-first reality: many users will be on mid-range Android phones.
