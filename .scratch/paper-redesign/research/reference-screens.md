# Reference screens for My Paper

Fetched 15 Aug 2026. Primary sources only: official product/help/marketing pages, plus two live personal sites. No blog recaps.

OpenPath context held while reading: My Paper is Drive then Docs; types are CV / cover letter / Proof; the personal link indexes published Proofs; no social feed. Drive is already locked as flat rows (name · type word · updated), not an A4 poster grid.

Steal / reject here is about the reference screen itself, not a build plan.

---

## 1. Resume.io and Kickresume

### Resume.io — the list of CVs

**URL:** [help.resume.io — duplicate / rename](https://help.resume.io/en/articles/3785344), [help.resume.io — download](https://help.resume.io/en/articles/3784704), [help.resume.io — customize](https://help.resume.io/en/articles/3784640), [help.resume.io — getting started](https://help.resume.io/en/articles/3785920)

**What it looks like.** After login you are on a **documents dashboard**, not inside a sheet. Each resume or cover letter is a titled row/card. Under the title sit actions: **Download PDF**, **Export to DOCX**, and **More** (copy, TXT). Hover the name and a **pencil** appears to rename in place; blur saves. A **flag** under the name changes the document language. **Tailor to the job listing** and **combine documents** also live on this dashboard, not in the builder. Premium users reach other career tools from a **Services** menu at the **top left** of the dashboard. Opening a document leaves this list; the list does not stay as a sidebar.

**Steal:** The list is a place you pick and file from. Export, duplicate, rename, and “this one is for a job” sit on the row. The sheet is not on stage until you open it.

**Reject:** A dashboard that also sells Job Tracker, Recruiter Match, Auto Apply, Interview Prep. The list is a career hub, not a quiet picker.

### Resume.io — form + live A4

**URL:** [help.resume.io — create a resume](https://help.resume.io/en/articles/3785152), [help.resume.io — preview](https://help.resume.io/en/articles/3785216), [help.resume.io — change template](https://help.resume.io/en/articles/3784960), [help.resume.io — customize](https://help.resume.io/en/articles/3784640)

**What it looks like.** Split builder. **Left:** stacked field boxes. Personal details are “boxes on the top left-hand side.” Employment, education, skills, then extra sections at the **bottom of the left column**. Hover a section: **drag dots** on the left of the title, **pencil** on the right to rename, **bin** on an entry to delete. Word-count hints sit **under** summary and employment. Skills offer a suggestion list. Spell-check underlines in red.

**Right:** a live resume preview. Changes show in real time. **Arrows at the bottom of the preview** turn page 2. **Line spacing** sits **at the top of the preview window** — you tighten the object, not the form. **Select template** sits **above the preview, right side**. Click **anywhere on the preview** and the sheet expands into a full template selector (bigger page, theme colours, spacing, page arrows, export still available). **Back to editor** returns to the split. The document **title** in the builder is click-to-rename.

You do not type on the A4. You fill the form; the sheet is the object you watch.

**Steal:** The page is a physical sheet: page arrows, spacing on the object, click-to-enlarge, export parked on the sheet. The form is how you write; the A4 is what you are making.

**Reject:** Thirty-plus skins in the selector, colour picker “any colour you’d like,” skill-level bars, photo slots as template chrome. The mall of finishes.

### Resume.io — export

**URL:** [help.resume.io — download](https://help.resume.io/en/articles/3784704), [help.resume.io — create a resume](https://help.resume.io/en/articles/3785152)

**What it looks like.** In the builder, a blue **Download PDF** sits **above the resume, top right**. Three dots beside it open DOCX, TXT, and **share a link**. The same exports exist under each title on the dashboard. Free plan: TXT only (no layout). Premium: PDF / Word, plus a share URL with view/download stats. The sheet you have been watching is the file you take.

**Steal:** Export is attached to the object (above the preview) and also to the list row. PDF is the real takeaway.

**Reject:** Paywall that strips layout from the free download. Link-plus-analytics as a product of its own.

### Resume.io — template wall (marketing)

**URL:** [resume.io](https://resume.io/), [resume.io/resume-templates](https://resume.io/resume-templates)

**What it looks like.** A dense gallery of named A4 thumbnails (Traditional, Professional, Prime ATS, Vancouver, Classic, …), each with a use-count. Same pattern on `/resume-templates`: Use this template + format badges (pdf / docx) + a one-line pitch. This is a **poster grid of sheets**.

**Steal:** A gallery of situations with a real A4 sense — the sheet is the tile.

**Reject:** Using this wall as the **document library**. It is how you choose a skin, not how you find last night’s CV.

### Kickresume — My Documents

**URL:** [kickresume.com/en/help-center/general](https://www.kickresume.com/en/help-center/general/), [kickresume.com/en/help-center/cover-letter](https://www.kickresume.com/en/help-center/cover-letter/), [kickresume.com/en](https://www.kickresume.com/en/)

**What it looks like.** Dashboard, then **My Documents** in the **left sidebar** (also in the top bar). Types are tabbed: **Resumes, Cover Letters, Resignation Letters, Websites**. Each document is a **preview card**. **Three dots sit in the bottom-right of the card** (Edit, Duplicate, Download PDF, Export Text to Word, Send to Email). **Click the preview** to enter the editor. Create is a **rectangle labelled Create New**; hover it for New / Use Example. **PDF Import** is a dashboard tile, not a row action. AI tools on the dashboard wear a **purple sparkle badge, top-left of the tile**.

**Steal:** Type is chosen in the list. Open means click the thing. Overflow lives on the card, not in a second toolbar.

**Reject:** The card **is** a mini A4. A populated My Documents is a poster grid of sheets. Also reject Resignation Letters and Websites as sibling types in the same picker, and the AI-badge tool mall above the list.

### Kickresume — form + live A4

**URL:** [kickresume.com/en/help-center/cover-letter](https://www.kickresume.com/en/help-center/cover-letter/), [kickresume.com/en/help-center/general](https://www.kickresume.com/en/help-center/general/), [kickresume.com/en/ai-resume-writer](https://www.kickresume.com/en/ai-resume-writer/)

**What it looks like.** **Left sidebar** of the editing interface: **Design → Template**, **Customization** (colour, font and size, **page format A4 or Letter**, line spacing, address format), **Download & Share**, **Proofreading**. **Above the resume preview, top right:** Examples, Guides, Help Center. New documents land in “the first template from the selection.” Matching cover-letter skins exist for resume templates. Section labels on the page are click-to-rewrite; **Edit labels** changes the rest. Premium controls wear a **blue pro badge**.

The sheet is still an object (A4/Letter is a Design setting; Examples sit *above the preview*), but more of the chrome is a left tool rail than Resume.io’s form/preview split.

**Steal:** Page size as a property of the sheet. Help parked above the preview, not in the form. Click-the-preview-to-open from the list.

**Reject:** Left-rail product (AI writer, proofreading, examples library of 2,200+). Matching resume/letter skin pairs. Pro badges on the page you are trying to finish tonight.

### Kickresume — export / share

**URL:** [kickresume.com/en/help-center/general](https://www.kickresume.com/en/help-center/general/)

**What it looks like.** Two places, same as Resume.io: card menu on the list, or **Download & Share** in the editor sidebar. Dashboard download is PDF. Editor also offers Word (plain text, “no graphics, no pictures”), email, Dropbox, Google Drive. **Share a Link** copies a live URL to an **online version of the same CV**, with a **Contact Me** button at the top; a toggle unpublishes it. The link is the document, not a separate site — until you use Websites (below).

**Steal:** PDF from the list or from beside the sheet. A share URL that is the current document.

**Reject:** Contact Me on the public CV. Word export that admits it throws the object away.

### What makes the sheet feel like an object

From the official chrome, not from reviews:

1. **It has pages.** Resume.io puts **arrows at the bottom of the preview** and again in the enlarged selector ([preview](https://help.resume.io/en/articles/3785216), [template](https://help.resume.io/en/articles/3784960)). You are holding a multi-page paper, not an infinite form.
2. **You manipulate the sheet, not only the fields.** Line spacing lives **on the preview** ([customize](https://help.resume.io/en/articles/3784640)). Clicking the preview **expands the page**. Kickresume’s **A4 vs Letter** is a Design control on the document ([cover letter help](https://www.kickresume.com/en/help-center/cover-letter/)).
3. **Export is bolted to the sheet.** Resume.io’s blue **Download PDF** is **above the resume, top right** ([download](https://help.resume.io/en/articles/3784704)). Kickresume’s Download & Share is a sidebar facing the preview ([general help](https://www.kickresume.com/en/help-center/general/)).
4. **The list shows the object (Kickresume) or refuses to (Resume.io).** Kickresume: click the preview. Resume.io: title + file actions, sheet appears only after open. Both treat the CV as a thing you take, not a settings page.

## 2. Google Docs — the page is the work

### Opening a doc from a list

**URL:** [support.google.com/docs — how to use](https://support.google.com/docs/answer/7068618), [support.google.com/docs — create / view / download](https://support.google.com/docs/answer/49114), [support.google.com/docs — screen reader](https://support.google.com/docs/answer/1632201), [support.google.com/drive — view & reorder](https://support.google.com/drive/answer/2375177)

**Docs home (`docs.google.com`).** Two bands. Top: **Start a new document** — a **Blank** thumbnail at top left, then a **Recently used templates** strip you arrow through. Below: a **recent documents** collection; screen-reader docs say initial focus is on that list, **Right arrow** to the next file, Enter to open. The home list **does not follow you in**. Opening replaces the picker with the document.

**Drive (`drive.google.com`).** Official default is **list layout** (name, sort columns). **Grid** is a toolbar toggle. Hovering a file can show a **preview card** (thumbnail, who last edited); that is optional and can be turned off in Settings. Folders, stars, colours, and a left nav (My Drive, Recent, Starred) are Drive, not Docs.

**What stays put when you open.** The picker is gone. New chrome: **document title at the top of the file** (click, type, Enter to rename; new files are “Untitled document”). **File / Edit / View / Insert / Format** menus. A format toolbar. **Share** toward the top right. Status is not a Save button — “every change is automatically saved in Drive.” Focus lands at the **start of the main editing area**. An **Outline** can be shown from View; it is off until asked. File > Open is a picker dialog (Recent / My Drive / Shared with me), not a persistent library rail.

**Steal:** List first, then a different chrome. The list does not cling as a sidebar. Title lives on the open document. Default Drive list is rows, not posters.

**Reject:** Docs home’s **template thumbnail strip** as a model for a paper library. Drive **grid + hover preview cards**. Folders, stars, and colour-coded filing. “Untitled document” as the first name.

### Chrome that gets out of the way

**URL:** [support.google.com/docs — keyboard shortcuts](https://support.google.com/docs/answer/179738), [support.google.com/docs — pages / pageless](https://support.google.com/docs/answer/11528737), [workspace.google.com/products/docs](https://workspace.google.com/products/docs/)

**What it looks like.** **Hide the menus (compact mode): Ctrl + Shift + F** (same chord on Mac). Menus recede; the page gets taller. Outline is a toggle, not furniture. **Pages** vs **Pageless** is File > Page setup: Pages keep headers, footers, page numbers, orientation, margins — a sheet. Pageless is continuous scroll; images and line breaks follow the window; View > Text width is Narrow / Medium / Wide for the *viewer*, not a shared layout. Official copy: pageless is for “continuously write and scroll without interruption.” Print still wants Pages.

**Steal:** Chrome that can leave. Typing is the point. A real page mode when the thing will be printed.

**Reject:** Pageless as the model for a sendable CV (no page numbers, no headers). Gemini, @ chips, tabs-inside-a-doc, comments, eSignature — team-doc chrome, not deadline-night paper.

### Typing on the page

**URL:** [support.google.com/docs — how to use](https://support.google.com/docs/answer/7068618), [support.google.com/docs — screen reader](https://support.google.com/docs/answer/1632201), [support.google.com/docs — download](https://support.google.com/docs/answer/49114)

**What it looks like.** You click the body and type. Double-click a word to select. Undo/Redo are at the top. There is no left-hand employment form. Navigation is through the text (character, word, line, paragraph, “displayed page, not a document page”). Download is **File > Download** (pick a type) or **File > Print → Save as PDF**. The work *is* the page.

**Steal:** For a letter (and a Proof dump), write on the surface. Export is File/Print, not a second layout engine.

**Reject:** Importing this as the CV builder. A CV in these products is a form that *paints* an A4; Docs is the opposite.

## 3. A personal / proof-like public page

Two live sites, plus Kickresume’s official “personal website” as the CMS control.

### Index of a person’s work — Frank Chimero, writing archive

**URL:** [frankchimero.com/blog](https://frankchimero.com/blog/), [frankchimero.com/about](https://frankchimero.com/about/), [frankchimero.com](https://frankchimero.com/)

**What it looks like.** `/blog/` is a **single column of titles and dates**, newest first, from 2025 back to 2009. No excerpts, no thumbnails, no tags, no like counts, no “follow.” `/about/` is a short bio in his voice, then **Experience** as job · role · years, then Interviews / Awards / Press / Speaking as quiet lists. The homepage fetch is almost only the name and “New York-based designer” — the person, not a dashboard. Nothing here is a feed.

**Steal:** An index can be a dated list of work. One voice. No engagement chrome. The page is a record, not a CMS home.

**Reject:** Turning the index into a template of sections (Bio, Skills, Projects, Contact) that every person gets.

### Index — Rauno Freiberg

**URL:** [rauno.me](https://rauno.me/)

**What it looks like.** One sentence: name, origin, role, where he works, one current thing (Devouring Details). A **Projects** heading. A line of maxims (“Make it fast. Make it beautiful…”). Featured: History of Software Design. No grid of identical cards, no follow button, no activity graph.

**Steal:** The index is an introduction plus a short list of work. Identity is a sentence, not a profile header with stats.

**Reject:** Desktop-OS theatre (dock, sounds, side-scrolling galleries) if the job is a calm personal link. Atmospheric chrome that competes with the work.

### One project page — Dust

**URL:** [frankchimero.com/projects/dust](https://frankchimero.com/projects/dust/)

**What it looks like.** Persistent name (“Frank Chimero / New York-based designer”) links home. Then **Dust**, one paragraph of intent (“collages gather old, forgotten images… ambiguity and wistfulness”), **2017–2020**, then a **vertical stack of images**. No sidebar of related posts, no template section tabs, no Contact Me, no “share this project.” The page is the work plus a reason it exists.

**Steal:** Title, optional dates, a short why, then the material. Photos allowed. The project is a designed page, not a resume section.

**Reject:** A case-study template (Challenge / Process / Outcome / Next). A comment thread. A related-work rail.

### One project page — Aaarrrppp

**URL:** [frankchimero.com/projects/arp](https://frankchimero.com/projects/arp/)

**What it looks like.** Same quiet chrome. A paragraph that says what the thing *is* (browser instrument, image → music, how it behaves). Then named pieces (Color Field, Blue Horse I, The Gulf Stream) — examples of the work, not CMS modules.

**Steal:** Specific, defensible description. The page can be unfinished (“work-in-progress”) without dummy section headings.

**Reject:** Auto-built “Projects” blocks cloned from a CV.

### What makes it feel considered rather than a CMS

Held against Kickresume’s own website product ([online-web](https://www.kickresume.com/en/online-web/), [templates/website](https://www.kickresume.com/en/templates/website/)):

| Considered (Chimero / Rauno) | CMS (Kickresume website) |
| --- | --- |
| One person’s words and pictures | “Turn your resume into a personal website with a single click” |
| Chrome is a name and a home link | 1 free + 6 premium templates; Design = background, fonts, date formats |
| Index is titles or a sentence | Same resume chunks (skills, experience, projects) plus graphs, certificates, strengths, publications |
| A project page has a reason and a date | Tabs or a sidebar to “toggle between your resume’s individual sections”; swipe horizontally on one template |
| No follow, no Contact Me slab | Official copy: “Contact Me” on the online CV; URL shaped `kickresume.com/cv/john-doe/` |
| You could not swap the template and keep the meaning | Official copy: “pre-designed web layouts that allow you to easily add, edit and arrange content” |

Considered = the page could only be this person’s. CMS = a skin over the same object.

**Steal (index):** Dated titles, or a short identity plus a list of published work. A record. No feed.

**Steal (project):** Title, optional role/dates/link, then the dump or the pictures. Designed, not a second CV.

**Reject:** Kickresume Websites (and any one-click “interactive resume”). Section tabs. Template families for the public person. Contact Me as default chrome. A social graph.

## Anything that would recreate an A4 poster-grid Drive

`docs/paper.md` already locked Drive as **flat rows**, no folders, no A4 poster grid. These official screens would drag that grid back:

1. **Resume.io template wall** — [resume.io](https://resume.io/), [resume.io/resume-templates](https://resume.io/resume-templates). A field of A4 thumbnails. Fine as a *create* gallery; fatal as the library of existing papers.
2. **Kickresume My Documents** — [cover letter help](https://www.kickresume.com/en/help-center/cover-letter/), [general help](https://www.kickresume.com/en/help-center/general/). Each paper is a **preview card**; you click the miniature sheet. That *is* a poster-grid library.
3. **Kickresume / Resume.io marketing template rows** — [kickresume.com/en/resumes](https://www.kickresume.com/en/resumes/) (Red, Puddle, Sunny, …). Same shape as (1).
4. **Google Docs home, “Start a new document”** — [how to use](https://support.google.com/docs/answer/7068618), [screen reader](https://support.google.com/docs/answer/1632201). A strip of **page-shaped thumbnails** (Blank + templates). Do not reuse as the Drive of built CVs.
5. **Google Drive grid + hover preview cards** — [view & reorder](https://support.google.com/drive/answer/2375177). Official default is **list**. Grid plus thumbnail cards is the poster library with a preview on hover.

If a reference shows a miniature A4 you click to open, it is teaching the Drive this repo already refused.
