Each tile **is a miniature sheet** — black on white, situation layout hinted (one column / two column / photo slot). Caption under the sheet: situation name, then CV or Resume, then photo flag. Design tile carries the honest warning. No accent picker. No density slider. No use-counts.

This is a **create wall**, not Drive. Drive stays a sheet-edge list. Resume.io’s template mall is the reference to steal *here* and reject *as the library*.

## Pick

Touch a sheet. That sheet **is** the origin. It travels into the Docs A4 (shared element). The other seven **Fade out**. You land on the output, on that situation’s structure, and you type on the paper.

Back from the wall returns to the stack (or the empty unmade object). Same room.
