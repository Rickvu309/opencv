## Question

How does the eight-situation gallery feel like a wall with a real A4 sense, and how do you enter Docs from a tile?

Keep: all eight at once; “Where is this CV going?”; word follows the template; photo flag; design-tile warning; accent and density stay out of the gallery (Format bar later).

### Advisor tokens

- Sheet `aspect-ratio: 1 / 1.414` (ISO 216 A4 portrait).
- Grid `gap` 16px (`--spacing` lg). Caption 8px under the sheet (intra-group). Two rows are one wall, not two sections.
- Sheet fill: paper-white. Hairline 1px. Hover/current: instant ink hairline, no `transition-colors` (Instant-Hover Rule). Focus: existing `ring-3 ring-ring/50`.
- Caption: situation name 14px body; CV/Resume · photo · warning 12px label. Warning is a line, not a colour alert.
- Whole tile is the hit. Sheet is far above 44px; caption rides along.
- No warm ink, no shadow-plus-border (Shadow-Ring Rule). Depth is the hairline and the A4 ratio.
