### Letter — standard paper, different object

| Asked | You chose |
| --- | --- |

| Chooser? | Four **situation cards**. No miniature A4s. “Who is this letter for?” |

| Export? | PDF via print, in the band. |

### Proof — a space, not a letter

### Advisor

- Letter: ~65ch measure, paper-white, hairline, leading edge + desk. Email: to / subject / body as lines on the page.
- Proof space: not an A4. Add always visible. Newest-first until they connect proofs.
- Add proof: **Reveal**. Letter typing: **instant**.
