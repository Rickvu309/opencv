## Question

What colour and type does My Paper earn, and why?

`DESIGN.md` (monochrome, scarce colour, Be Vietnam Pro, Kumo craft) is evidence of the current system, not a lock. The spec may propose a new craft for Drive, Docs, the document sheet, the published Proof page, and the personal link. Documents may stay black-on-white even if chrome does not. Proof may look hotter than a CV.

Every proposed colour or type change needs a reason a deadline-night student can feel — not decoration. Use `/grilling`, `/domain-modeling`, and `/impeccable` (colorize / typeset). Read [Reference screens for My Paper](01-reference-screens.md) first. Do not build.

## Answer

Locked 15 Aug 2026. Written into [spec.md](../spec.md) (Craft, and Upload as a starting point). Follow-up: [Upload as a starting point](11-upload-as-a-starting-point.md).

### Round 1 — colour and type

| Asked | You chose |
| --- | --- |
| What is colour for? | **Room vs output.** A second neutral (and one accent) separates the desk from the paper you send. Not type-coding. Not delight paint. Not status-only. |
| How far does the Drive room move? | **Keep the desk.** Light room, hairlines. The A4 is paper-white, a different surface. One accent on New and the unmade CV only. Not a dark-room fork. Not a second product palette. |
| May the A4 leave black-on-white? | **No.** CV and letter stay black ink on white paper in every theme. |
| Proof and the personal link? | **Published Proof and `/u/[handle]` may be hotter.** The Proof dump in Docs stays a working canvas. |
| Type? | **Chrome: one product sans** (Be Vietnam Pro unless replaced). **The A4 may wear a second face**; template can change it. No display type in the band. |
