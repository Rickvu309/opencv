# Published Proof page

Type: prototype
Status: resolved
Blocked by: 04, 07

## Question

How does the Proof space become a designed share page at `/p/[slug]` — smart, attractive, rich (and funny when the voice is human)?

## Answer

Locked 15 Aug 2026. Spec: [Published Proof](../spec.md#published-proof). Artifact: [published-proof-outline.md](../research/published-proof-outline.md). **Prepare** is retired on the band. Glossary still says Prepare until the spec is accepted.

### The two steps

| Asked | You chose |
| --- | --- |
| Band verb? | **Publish** — puts the page on the web. Not Share (OS). Not Prepare (the old rail). |
| Steps? | **Two.** **Arrange** is step 1: they live on a **draft of the real page** (pick, why, order — they see the outcome). **Publish** is step 2. |
| Leave without publishing? | **Yes.** The draft stays on this Proof. The space is unchanged. |

### The page

| Asked | You chose |
| --- | --- |
| What goes on it? | What they pick in Arrange. The space may be fuller. |
| Layout? | **One considered system** — title, why, dates, then material. No template mall. |
| Order? | **Theirs**, set in Arrange. Not newest-first. |
| Photos? | **Large, in the flow.** Not a thumb grid. |
| Connections? | Quiet “this belongs with that.” No node graph as the page. |
| Voice? | **Theirs.** No auto-witty captions. Coach may be a quiet offer on the why. |
| Chrome? | **Name → personal link.** Quiet footer. No Contact Me, no follow, no OpenPath hero. |
| After publish? | Space still editable. Arrange again, then Publish again. **Not live-sync.** |
| Hard-work mark? | May appear, quiet, once [Personal link](09-personal-link.md) picks the shape. |
| Unpublish? | From Docs, on this Proof. Space stays. |

### Advisor

- Public page may leave app chrome (hotter). Type can be a second face. Contrast still ≥4.5:1 on body.
- Measure ~65ch for the why; photos can bleed wider than the text.
- Draft in Docs uses the same layout as `/p/[slug]`, on the desk, with Arrange chrome (pick / why / order) around it — not a second builder route.
