# Accessibility

Source: Accessibility, https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/accessibility, read 2026-11-13 through its `.md` copy.

Kinds: `machine` a command fails or reports it, `eye` look at a named screenshot, `judgement` decide and say why. Carry: `as is`, `adapted` (web equivalent named), see `left-out.md` for what was dropped.

| ID  | Guideline sentence (quoted)                                                                                                                                                                    | Carry   | Web rule                                                                                                                                                | Kind      | Check                                                                                                     |
| --- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| A1  | "Strive to meet color contrast minimum standards." Table: "Up to 17 pts / All / 4.5:1", "18 pts / All / 3:1", "All / Bold / 3:1".                                                              | adapted | 1pt = 1 CSS px. Text under 18px and lighter than 700 needs 4.5:1 against the composited background; 18px or 700 needs 3:1. Icon-only controls need 3:1. | machine   | sweep rule `hig-contrast`                                                                                 |
| A3  | "Convey information with more than color alone."                                                                                                                                               | as is   | State (error, selected, unread, approved) carries a word, icon or shape besides its colour.                                                             | eye       | in each shot, cover the colour: can you still tell which item is selected or failed?                      |
| A4  | "Support larger text sizes."                                                                                                                                                                   | adapted | Dynamic Type becomes the browser's text-size setting: rem-based type that grows, and layouts that hold at 200%.                                         | machine   | sweep rule `hig-text-size`                                                                                |
| A5  | "Use recommended defaults for custom type sizes." Table: "iOS, iPadOS / 17 pt / 11 pt".                                                                                                        | adapted | Body text 15 to 17px at phone width; nothing under 11px.                                                                                                | eye       | `node scripts/ui.mjs measure <screen>` lists font sizes; flag any under 11px                              |
| A6  | "Bear in mind that font weight can also impact how easy text is to read."                                                                                                                      | as is   | No 100 to 300 weights under 18px.                                                                                                                       | eye       | `measure` weight column                                                                                   |
| A7  | "Offer sufficiently sized controls." Table: "iOS, iPadOS / 44x44 pt / 28x28 pt".                                                                                                               | adapted | 44 by 44 CSS px at phone width (430px and under).                                                                                                       | machine   | sweep rule `fits` (rendered size) and `scripts/touch-targets.test.mjs` (class names)                      |
| A8  | "Consider spacing between controls as important as size." "about 12 points of padding around elements that include a bezel ... about 24 points" without one.                                   | adapted | 12px between bordered or filled controls, 24px around bare icon buttons.                                                                                | eye       | phone shot: two tappable things closer than 12px                                                          |
| A9  | "Offer alternatives to gestures."                                                                                                                                                              | as is   | Every drag, swipe or long press has a button or menu path.                                                                                              | judgement | name the button that does the same as each gesture                                                        |
| A10 | "Let people use the keyboard alone to navigate and interact with your app."                                                                                                                    | adapted | Every control reachable by Tab, operable by Enter or Space, with a visible focus ring.                                                                  | machine   | sweep rules `hig-focus-ring` and `focus` (Escape returns focus); `dialogs-trap-tab.test.tsx`              |
| A11 | "Describe your app's interface and content for VoiceOver."                                                                                                                                     | adapted | Every control has an accessible name; icon-only buttons carry `aria-label`.                                                                             | judgement | read names in `node scripts/ui.mjs measure <screen>`; deeper rules live in the better-accessibility skill |
| A12 | "Minimize use of time-boxed interface elements." "Prefer dismissing views with an explicit action."                                                                                            | as is   | A toast that carries an action (Undo) stays until dismissed or long enough to reach.                                                                    | eye       | open the action and time the toast; `toast-position.test.mjs` covers placement only                       |
| A13 | "Be cautious with fast-moving and blinking animations." "ensure your app or game responds by reducing automatic and repetitive animations, including zooming, scaling, and peripheral motion." | adapted | With `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`, nothing repeats or moves; fades stay.                                                                            | machine   | sweep rule `hig-reduced-motion` (the sweep's browser asks for reduced motion)                             |
| A14 | "Let people control audio and video playback."                                                                                                                                                 | as is   | No autoplaying media.                                                                                                                                   | judgement | the product has none today; say so if you add some                                                        |
| A15 | "Keep actions simple and intuitive."                                                                                                                                                           | as is   | One way to do a common thing, the same on every screen.                                                                                                 | judgement | compare with the sibling screen                                                                           |
| A16 | "Augment audio cues with visual cues."                                                                                                                                                         | as is   | Any sound has a visible twin.                                                                                                                           | judgement | the product makes no sound today                                                                          |
