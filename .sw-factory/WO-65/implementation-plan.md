# Implementation Plan: WO-65

**Work Order:** WO-65 - Rebuild the landing page to sell the vision

## Why the old page was not enough

Three short declarations and a waiting list. It read well and it sold nothing:
"hiểu bản thân, chọn đúng hướng, biết bắt đầu từ đâu" is what every careers
service in the country says, and none of it tells the reader that the Coach
here writes the CV rather than advising them to write one. That difference is
the product, and it was invisible.

## What the page now says

- **Audience before headline.** A student scanning this needs to know it is for
  them before deciding whether the promise is worth reading (AC-LAND-001.4).
- **The promise, not a tagline**: ready for work worth doing (AC-LAND-001.1).
- **The differentiator, in the subhead**: the Coach writes, sharpens and finds,
  and you stay in control (AC-LAND-001.2).
- **Two pillars in student words** (AC-LAND-001.3): get yourself ready, then
  find the right place. Not "personal branding" and "job discovery".
- **How it works**, three concrete steps, below the fold on purpose: somebody
  convinced by the hero should not scroll past an explanation to reach the
  button (AC-LAND-003.1).
- **The control promise**, stated plainly at the end, because it is both the
  differentiator and the thing a reader is right to be suspicious of.

## What is deliberately absent

No testimonials, no "10,000 students", no logos of companies nobody placed
anyone at. AC-LAND-003.2 asks for honesty, and the product's first principle is
evidence over encouragement: a landing page that opens with an invented number
has broken that promise before the visitor signs up.

## Copy

Written per language rather than translated, and both languages inside the
repo's word budget. The budget test caught six entries over it, including the
subhead, and they were shortened rather than exempted. The shorter subhead is
better copy.

The waitlist route and store are reused unchanged, as the work order requires.
