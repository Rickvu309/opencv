# Apply

The jobs a person is going after.

## Language

**Job**:
One role at one employer that the person is going after. It has two halves that will part ways: the Role and the Pursuit.
_Avoid_: opportunity, application, position

**Role**:
The employer's side of a Job: title, company, place and posting. The same for everyone.

**Pursuit**:
The person's side of a Job: stage, priority, notes, checklist, dates and the Papers sent. Private to them.

**Stage**:
Where a Job stands: saved, applied, interview, offer or closed.
_Avoid_: status, column

**Posting**:
The employer's own text for a Job.
_Avoid_: job description, ad

**Checklist**:
The person's to-do items for one Job.

**Priority**:
How much a Job matters to the person: low, normal or high.
