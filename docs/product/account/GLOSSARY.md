# Account

Who the person is, how they sign in and what plan they are on.

## Language

**Person**:
The human who uses OpenCV.
_Avoid_: user, customer, owner

**Profile**:
What OpenCV knows about a person's career: experience, skills and preferences. The Coach grounds on it.
_Avoid_: account, settings

**Account**:
A person's sign-in identity and plan.
_Avoid_: profile, user

**Plan**:
What a person pays for. Free or Pro.
_Avoid_: tier, mode
