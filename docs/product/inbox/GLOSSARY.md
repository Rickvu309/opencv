# Inbox

Recruiter mail, tied to Jobs.

## Language

**Mail connection**:
A person's permission for OpenCV to read and send from one mailbox.
_Avoid_: connection, integration

**Thread**:
One mail conversation with a recruiter.
_Avoid_: chat

**Suggestion**:
What OpenCV thinks a thread means for a Job, such as a move to interview.

**Draft**:
A reply written for the person. It is sent only after their click.
