# Space

A public directory of people, companies and roles. Nothing here is built. The terms are kept so today's words do not block it.

## Language

**Company**:
An employer with its own page. Today it is only a name written on a Job.

**Public profile**:
What a person chooses to show the world. It can exist before the person joins and be claimed later.
_Avoid_: profile, which is private

**Cosign**:
One person's public statement of belief in another person or a company, with the reason. A kind of Proof.
_Avoid_: endorsement, recommendation, review

**List**:
A person's named collection of people or companies.
