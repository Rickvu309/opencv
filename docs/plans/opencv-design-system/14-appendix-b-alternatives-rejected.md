## Appendix B. Alternatives rejected, and defaults applied

- B1. Move to a Radix style. Rejected. No named registry needs it, Cult Halo pieces would still bring Base UI, and it re-skins every control at once (section "Keep base-nova on Base UI").
- B2. Keep the serif display heading. Default applied is to drop it, because `--font-heading` is part of the ADR-0010 overlay and the operator ruled against building on it. This is a voice call only the operator can make. If the operator wants the serif back, ADR-0014 can name one heading font set through the CLI's own theme output rather than a bespoke token, and DS-1 keeps `font-heading` out of the forbidden list.
- B3. Streamdown for Coach markdown. Rejected for now. `coach-markdown.tsx` already renders the Coach's markdown and has tests, and Streamdown adds a dependency the Message page recommends but does not require (A3 Message).
- B4. Kibo `table` for Paper and Kibo `calendar` for Apply. Rejected. The Paper list is a handful of rows, which stock `table` covers without `@tanstack/react-table` and `jotai` (A6). Apply's calendar is not named in the operator's DS-3 scope and the research does not ask for it. Filed as a follow-up candidate.
- B5. Showpiece pieces from Cult UI, Magic UI or affectUI on Paper and Pitch. Not added. Research section 4 says "No showpiece animation on these four surfaces. The work is the showpiece." The operator's instruction scopes showpieces to "where the research names a showpiece", and it names none. Those registries stay available for the landing page, under its own verify-landing gate.
- B6. Coach Confirmation wired to writes. Deferred. The api has no approval state. DS-2 copies the pattern, DS-4a uses it for Paper suggestions, and the Coach wiring is its own issue.
- B7. Fix the overlay per surface instead of a codemod. Rejected. About 70 micro-tier and 40 role or motion hits across roughly 35 files are mechanical. A re-runnable `scripts/ds-codemod.mjs` is cheaper to review and reruns after every registry install.
